# Video index (paper_261114)

> These videos are a demonstration of the navigation-side configuration frozen in Stage 1 (Comfort costmap buffer 0.10 m; identical safety layer, collision monitor and Nav2 parameters for B2 and B3-O) applied to the existing complex cross scenario (8 people, AMR 1/2/3). They are **not** results of the Stage 3 main final performance experiment, which needs the Stage 2 human study (R_comfort_min, R_pref are still null). Comfort sizes are the ones of the existing diagnostic pair (Static r 1.20 / B3-O target 1.20), not Dynamic Selection.

Frames are taken at 15 fps on the simulation clock and resampled to 30 fps (simulated real time), 1920x1080 (side-by-sides wider). Overlays are drawn from the per-frame pose log of the same run.

Total 69 videos: clean 15, overlay 54. Demo/capture runs executed: 8 in results/videos/paper_261114/demo_runs (not a results batch); 7 used (a re-recorded tag supersedes its earlier run). Also reused: today's capture runs under results/captures/paper_261114/capture_trials (corridor buffer comparison, corridor ProtectiveStop, cross buffer-0 diagnostic pairs).

Top views: every scenario has a fixed orthographic top view (CAM01_TOP; corridor 13 m wide). The corridor and buffer-0 cross capture runs, first recorded with one perspective camera, were re-recorded with the top camera in results/videos/paper_261114/top_runs (13 runs; same condition and base files, capture runs, not a results batch). A re-recording's outcome is its own and can differ from the earlier capture run; the caption of each clip shows the verdict of the run it shows.
